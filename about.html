Our Story

Who We Are

Rooted in Compassion, Driven by Youth

NayePankh Foundation was founded in 2021 by Prashant Shukla, with a vision to serve society. What started as a small student-led initiative in Saharanpur, Uttar Pradesh, has grown into one of India's biggest student-led NGOs impacting thousands of lives.

Registered under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860 by the Government of Uttar Pradesh (Reg. No: KAP/04397/2021-2022), we are also approved under Section 12A and 80G of the Income Tax Act — making your donations tax-deductible.
