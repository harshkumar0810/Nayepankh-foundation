NayePankh Foundation has been working since 2021 for under and less privileged people in the field of hunger, sanitary, health, education, awareness and rights.
NayePankh Foundation promotes the culture of kindness and wants to instill the sense of giving back to the society amongst modern youth.
NayePankh Foundation works with a vision to create a society where children can prosper to their complete potential and enjoy equality in its true essence.
Our Focus

What We Stand For

Six pillars of change — every initiative rooted in compassion and grassroots action.

Hunger Relief

Regular food distribution drives ensuring no one sleeps hungry in the communities we serve.

Education

School bags, books, pens and stationery distributed to underprivileged children across UP.

Health & Sanitation

Health camps, sanitary pad drives, and hygiene awareness for women and girls.

Environment

Tree plantation drives and plastic-free campaigns across villages in Uttar Pradesh.

Youth & Culture

Cultural programs — dance, song, drama — giving students a platform to showcase talent.

Awareness & Rights

Community awareness programs on social rights, equality, and civic responsibility.
