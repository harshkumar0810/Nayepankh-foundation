Common Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes! NayePankh Foundation is approved under Section 80G (URN: AAGAN9381EF20227). You'll receive a tax exemption certificate with your donation receipt.

How can I volunteer?

Visit our Programs page and fill out the volunteer form, or email us at hello@nayepankh.org. We welcome volunteers across Uttar Pradesh.

Where do you work?

Our registered office is in Kanpur Nagar, UP. We actively work in Saharanpur, Kanpur and surrounding villages across Uttar Pradesh.

How is donation money used?

100% of donations go directly to our programs — food drives, education kits, sanitary pad distribution, and tree plantation. We are 80G & 12A registered for full transparency.
